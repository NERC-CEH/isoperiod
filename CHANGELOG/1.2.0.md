# 1.2.0

A minor release adding two properties for rendering a `Period` in English words.

## Features

### `verbose` and `descriptive`

`Period.verbose` spells a period's duration out in full, appending its offset or origin in brackets. `descriptive`
names the period's frequency with a common word where one exists (e.g. "Daily", "Yearly"), falling back to the plain
duration otherwise, and recognises two grids by name: the UK Water Day (`P1D+T9H`) and the UK Water Year (`P1Y+9MT9H`).

Examples:

|                               | `verbose`                    | `descriptive`                |
|-------------------------------|------------------------------|------------------------------|
| `Period.of_years(1)`          | `"1 year"`                   | `"Yearly"`                   |
| `Period.of_minutes(15)`       | `"15 minutes"`               | `"15 minutes"`               |
| `Period.of("P1D+T9H")`        | `"1 day (+9 hours)"`         | `"Daily (UK Water Day)"`     |
| `Period.of("2024-01-01/P7D")` | `"7 days (from 2024-01-01)"` | `"Weekly (from 2024-01-01)"` |

Both are for display only: neither is accepted by `Period.of` or any other constructor, and `str()` remains the
parseable form.
